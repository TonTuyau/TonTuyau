# TonTuyau 2.0.0

**TonTuyau stands on its own.** It keeps its library itself, downloads from YouTube itself, and plays with its own
player: TubeArchivist is no longer needed, only read once if you come from it. And TonTuyau is now free software,
under the GPL 3 or later, with its browser extension [LaPompe](https://github.com/TonTuyau/LaPompe) in the Chrome Web
Store and Firefox Add-ons.

## The library is TonTuyau's

- **In PostgreSQL**: every video and channel, what YouTube said of them, their state on YouTube, their categories, the
  comments read. Elasticsearch keeps only the search indices, written from PostgreSQL: `tontuyau index rebuild` never
  needs anything else. A video's or a channel's page reads PostgreSQL itself.
- **TubeArchivist is an import.** With `TONTUYAU_TA_URL` and `TONTUYAU_TA_TOKEN` set, each start reads its whole
  library (read-only, never changed) and adds what TonTuyau doesn't have: videos, channels, their pictures and
  subscriptions. What TonTuyau has stays as it is, nothing is ever deleted. Once it ran, TubeArchivist can be shut
  down. Also from the Tasks page and `tontuyau import`. TubeArchivist's own syncs, schedule, deletions and their
  settings are gone.
- **The runs, the lock and their logs** are in PostgreSQL too.

## TonTuyau downloads

- **Download a video**: from the Tasks page, LaPompe, or `tontuyau youtube download <id or link>` (watch, youtu.be,
  shorts, live, embed links). yt-dlp downloads it with the format of your choice (`bestvideo+bestaudio` by default),
  merged by ffmpeg into one `.mkv` with its chapters, into `<channel id>/<video id>.mkv` of the videos folder,
  through a hidden folder first so a half-downloaded file never shows. Its channel is added when it's new. A video
  of the library whose file is missing gets its file back.
- **Subscriptions**: **Add a channel** (by id, `@handle` or link; the Tasks page, LaPompe, or
  `tontuyau youtube add-channel`) subscribes to its videos, Shorts and streams, the tabs you tick. A channel's admin
  page changes them, or unsubscribes. The import brings TubeArchivist's subscriptions along.
- **The YouTube syncs**: every 6 hours by itself (a setting, 0 for on demand only), each subscribed channel is listed,
  newest first, down to the first video the library has, and what's new is downloaded. The **full sync** lists them
  whole and downloads everything missing; **Download everything** on a channel's admin page does it for that channel
  alone. Streams still to come wait until they're over.
- **Failed downloads** are kept with why (members only, no such format…), on the channel's admin page, with Try again
  and Try them all again. After 3 failures the regular sync leaves one; the full sync still tries it.
- **Refresh everything**: every channel and its videos read again on YouTube, the least recently read first, skipping
  what was read in the last days (7 by default). Days for a big library; stopped, the next one goes on with what's
  left.
- **The videos folder is written to**: the backend runs as `TONTUYAU_UID`:`TONTUYAU_GID` (1654:1654 by default), to
  take the folder's group, or its owner too. What it writes is group-writable (folders 775, files 664); TubeArchivist's
  files are never changed.

## One queue for every task

- **A task started while another runs waits its turn** instead of being refused: the import, the downloads, the
  syncs, the refreshes all go through one queue, first come first served, kept across restarts. The Tasks page shows
  it, who asked and when, with Cancel; the command line queues and follows its run.
- **On hold, not failed, when YouTube refuses** (a bot check, too many requests): the run stops asking at once, waits
  for a cool-down, and resumes from where it was; **Resume now** and **Cancel** work while others wait behind it. The
  queue waits for it.

## LaPompe, the browser extension

[LaPompe](https://github.com/TonTuyau/LaPompe) puts **Add to TonTuyau** next to Subscribe on YouTube's video and channel
pages, and says when TonTuyau has them already ("In TonTuyau", "Subscribed in TonTuyau", "Waiting in TonTuyau"). It
also keeps TonTuyau signed in to YouTube, by sending it your youtube.com cookie now and then.
[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lapompe/cbijokghilmbekhefojdfoipjiefmoio),
[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/lapompe/).

## TonTuyau's own player

- **Its own controls** over the video: the bar with the chapters as segments (hovered: the chapter and the time), the
  volume (remembered), the chapter playing (a click lists them), a menu with the speed and loop, small or large, full
  screen. A click plays or pauses, a double click goes full screen, a tap on a phone too; Space, ←/→, ↑/↓, M and F.
  The video starts by itself.
- **Fetched about 30 s ahead**, rather than the whole file as fast as the connection goes: the video is played in
  parts of about 6 s, from keyframe to keyframe, each copied out of the file by ffmpeg when the player asks for it. No
  re-encoding, nothing written to disk, the original file untouched; seeking starts a new copy from there. MP4 and
  Matroska alike, in Chrome, Firefox and Edge; where the parts can't play, the whole file, as before. Lighter on a slow
  upload or a tunnel.

## Around the app

- **New icons**: Google's Material Symbols, Rounded, outlined, the current page's filled: not the icons YouTube's look
  comes from.
- **The admin pages say less, and explain on demand**: a "?" at the top right of each zone opens its explanation.
- **The dashboard** loads its channels a page at a time (sorted, filtered by name); its import history and its
  "Library updated" line count TonTuyau's downloads as the imports.
- The login's eye shows the password as typed; the admin pages use up to 1600 px of a wide screen; a channel's
  refresh asked for during a run joins the queue.

## Underneath

- **ffmpeg and ffprobe in the backend image**: a static FFmpeg 9.0.2 (LGPL), pinned by its checksum. They merge the
  downloads and copy the player's parts.
- **yt-dlp's unpacked copy** (about 85 MB in the temporary folder at each start) no longer stays behind when yt-dlp is
  stopped.
- The API contract went from v1.16 to v2.10 ([docs/API_CONTRACT.md](https://github.com/TonTuyau/TonTuyau/blob/v2.0.0/docs/API_CONTRACT.md)).
- CI checks the backend and the front side by side, the backend only when a pull request concerns it, and each version
  tag gets its GitHub Release.

## Upgrading from 1.x

**Start from an empty TonTuyau, and import again.** 2.0 keeps the library in new PostgreSQL tables and doesn't read
what 1.x stored:

1. `docker compose down`, then remove PostgreSQL's and Elasticsearch's volumes:
   `docker volume rm tontuyau_tontuyau-db-data tontuyau_tontuyau-es-data`.
2. Take the new `docker-compose.yml` and `.env.example`. The `TONTUYAU_SYNC_*` settings are gone; keep
   `TONTUYAU_TA_URL` and `TONTUYAU_TA_TOKEN` to import; check `TONTUYAU_UID` and `TONTUYAU_GID` against your videos
   folder, which is now written to (see the README's "The videos folder").
3. `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`: the admin account is created again from `TONTUYAU_ADMIN_PASSWORD`,
   and the import at startup fills the library. The pictures (`tontuyau-art`) stay and are reused.
