# TonTuyau 2.0.1

**The Shorts get TonTuyau's own player too**, as YouTube's Shorts: they were the last place still showing the
browser's own controls.

- **A thin bar along the bottom edge** of the short, always there, in TonTuyau's teal: it thickens under the pointer,
  and a click or a drag goes there. A drag along it is never taken for a swipe to the next short.
- **Play and the volume at the top left**, shown while the short is paused, while the pointer moves over it, and for a
  moment after a tap on a phone (where the volume is the phone's own buttons: only mute). The volume is the watch
  page's: set on one, it's the same on the other.
- **A click or a tap on the short plays or pauses**, as on the watch page; a short still starts by itself and loops.
- **The keyboard**: Space plays or pauses, ←/→ go back or forward 5 s, M mutes; ↑/↓ still go to the short before and
  after.
- The title and the channel stay over the bottom of the picture at all times: they no longer step aside for the
  browser's controls.
