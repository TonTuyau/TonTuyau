# TonTuyau 2.1.0

**Easier to read, on every screen**: TonTuyau scales up on big screens, its buttons speak one language, the watch page
gives the video the whole width, and the YouTube sync can run as eagerly as every minute.

## Big screens

- **The whole interface scales up on QHD, 4K and 5K screens**: text, icons, buttons, thumbnails and spacing together,
  a quarter more from 2560 × 1440 (the 1440p ultrawides too), half more on 4K, twice on 5K and up. Width and height
  both count, so a wide but short screen isn't blown up; Full HD and below look as before.
- The thumbnails are fetched at the size they're shown, so they stay sharp once scaled.
- A browser's own larger default text size now scales the whole interface, not only the text.

## The watch page

- **No menu docked on the left**, at any screen size, as on YouTube: the video and its side column have the room.
  ☰ opens the menu over the page; Esc, a click beside it or one of its links closes it. Every other page keeps its
  menu as it was.
- **The chapters are set apart from the description**: still in its box, after a line, under a heading with their
  count ("Chapters · 5"), so they no longer read as its last paragraph.
- The playing chapter's outline is even all around (it was cut at the top and on the first chapter's left).

## Buttons

- **Two kinds, the same on every page**: a zone's main action in TonTuyau's teal, every other action in grey. They
  came in four looks (black or white, grey, outlined, teal).
- **Easier to spot in the dark theme**: the grey buttons are much lighter than the cards they sit on (they all but
  vanished), and the teal one is a touch deeper, so its text reads well on any background.
- The top bar's "Log in" stays as it is.

## The YouTube sync, in minutes

- **"Run it by itself every" takes minutes or hours**, from 1 minute to a week, for whoever wants new videos as soon
  as they're out (0 still means on demand only). A whole number of hours shows in hours, as the default 6.
- The upgrade keeps each one's interval (6 hours becomes 360 minutes).
- API: the YouTube settings' `syncEveryHours` becomes `syncEveryMinutes`, 0 to 10080 (contract v2.11).

## Shorts

- The Shorts player setting reads **Standard** (the vertical player) and **Landscape** (the watch page); in French
  Standard and Paysage. Everyone's choice stays as it was.

## For developers

- The mockup can show the admin pages: `/__mock` → "log in as admin", and the dashboard, Tasks, YouTube, a channel's
  admin page and the API keys work on the synthetic library, their actions included.
