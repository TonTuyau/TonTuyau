# TonTuyau 2.1.1

Small things that make TonTuyau nicer to use: a real theater view, clearer admin pages, a lighter search bar.

## Watching

- **The large player is a theater view**, as YouTube's: the whole width of the window, from the top bar down, on
  black, without padding nor round corners. It's as tall as the window leaves with the video's title in sight, but
  never taller than the video at that width, so a narrow window doesn't get a tall black box. The details and the
  channel's other videos stay below, in their usual column. Switching between the two sizes doesn't restart the video.
- **The search bar has no magnifying glass on its left** any more: the text starts at the field's edge, the search
  button keeps its own.

## Administration

- **Filtering keeps the page where it is**: choosing a filter of the Tasks page's history (and of a channel's videos,
  the feeds and the channels list) no longer sends the page back to its top.
- **"Its videos on YouTube"**, on a channel's admin page, is easier to read: the counts in a column of their own,
  right-aligned beside their state, a zero dimmed.
- **"Follow its log" looks like a link** where it's part of a sentence (after "Try them all again", or while a run is
  going on): it took the sentence's colour before.
- **The dashboard's channels no longer have a "Go to channel" button**: a channel opens its admin page, which leads to
  its page in TonTuyau.
