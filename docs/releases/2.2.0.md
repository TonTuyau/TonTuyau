# TonTuyau 2.2.0

**See where the space goes**: the dashboard shows the videos folder's disk and what TonTuyau's videos take on it, each
channel says its size, and the downloads can wait instead of filling the disk.

## Storage on the dashboard

- **A "Storage" zone**, under the play time and the import history: the free space of the videos folder's disk first,
  big ("412 GB free", "of 1 TB"), then the disk as a bar: TonTuyau's videos by type in their colours, the disk's other
  files in grey (what isn't TonTuyau's, e.g. what TubeArchivist left), the free space outlined. Each part says its
  size and its share of the disk, the types their number of files.
- **Under the threshold, a warning**: "Under 50 GB free: the downloads don't run until space is freed", or "…: the
  next downloads may not fit" when they're set to go on.
- A disk that doesn't say its free space (some shares) shows TonTuyau's videos alone, without a warning.

## Each channel's size

- **A "Size" column** at the end of the dashboard's channels, sortable as the others: the biggest channels first in a
  click.
- **A channel's admin page says its size** under "In TonTuyau": the total, and by type (the types it has files of).

## The downloads wait for disk space

- **Two settings on the YouTube page**, under "Downloading" → "Disk space": "Warn under [50] GB free on the videos
  folder's disk" (0: never), and "Below it, don't run the downloads" (on by default).
- **Below it, with the switch on**, the YouTube syncs, a channel's "Download everything", "Download a video" (from
  LaPompe too) and "Try again" don't start: the Tasks page says why, with the free space. The scheduled sync waits,
  and starts by itself once there's room.
- **A sync going on looks before each download**: once under the threshold, it goes on hold where it was. It tries
  again by itself after the YouTube page's cool-down (and holds again while there's no room), or at once with Resume
  now.
- The refreshes, the import and "Add a channel" don't download anything: they still run.
- The upgrade starts everyone at 50 GB with the switch on: a disk already under it holds the downloads at once. Lower
  the threshold, or turn the switch off, on the YouTube page.

## For developers

- API (contract v2.12): `GET /api/admin/dashboard` gains `storage` (`disk`, `types`, `minFree`, `holdDownloads`), the
  dashboard's channels `size` and `sort=size`, a channel's admin page `size` by type; the YouTube settings
  `minFreeGb` (0 to 1,000,000) and `holdBelowMinFree`. A download refused for the disk space is a 409 saying why.
- The mockup's admin side sets the disk: `/__mock` → "Disk": roomy, almost full, or not saying its free space.
